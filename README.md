### 📌 Featured Projects

**[Voice-to-Email Automation](https://github.com/YujinPri/voice-to-email-n8n)**
Voice memo in, ready-to-send email draft out. A single n8n workflow with Groq Whisper transcription, a 3-model OpenRouter fallback chain, and anti-hallucination guardrails with self-reported confidence. Runs entirely on free-tier services.

**[LEVER: Automation Agency Site](https://github.com/YujinPri/lever-site)** · [Live](https://leverso.vercel.app)
Marketing site with a real database-backed booking calendar. Timezone-aware slots, and double-booking made impossible at the database level with a partial unique index. Next.js, Neon Postgres, Drizzle.

**[Personal Portfolio](https://eugene-privaldos.vercel.app)**
My portfolio site with an AI assistant chat and a live community board (sticky notes, group chat, presence). Next.js, Groq, Upstash Redis, deployed on Vercel.
